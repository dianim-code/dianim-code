### About me

I came to data engineering through a biomedical engineering background — spent a few years analyzing electrograms and ECG signals before realizing I liked the "finding structure in messy systems" part more than the "systems" part. That instinct carried straight over: today I build pipelines and BI platforms instead of signal-processing models, but it's the same job — turn noisy inputs into something trustworthy.

I'm currently a **Data Engineer at ELFO Srl**, and before that spent 4+ years as a Senior Consultant at BIP, working mostly with Pharma and Public Sector clients on:

- 🏗️ Replacing legacy Excel simulators with interactive **Power BI** "what-if" models for sales/forecasting planning
- 🐍 Building **Python** engines for automated ERP reconciliation (fuzzy-matching thousands of transactions)
- ☁️ Architecting scalable pipelines across **Microsoft Fabric** and **GCP**
- 🗺️ Governed geospatial data infrastructure for civil protection / land-use monitoring
- 🔒 Enforcing RLS/OLS security and automating ETL between SAP, Oracle, and BI layers
